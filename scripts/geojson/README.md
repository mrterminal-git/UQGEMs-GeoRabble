# Brisbane boundaries

`brisbane_wards.geojson` contains Brisbane City Council's 26 electoral wards.
`brisbane_lga.geojson` is their dissolved outer boundary. Both are produced by
`scripts/download_brisbane_boundaries.py` from the Queensland Government's
Brisbane City Council ward ArcGIS layer.

The source layer states that the 2020 ward boundaries remained unchanged for
the 2024 quadrennial local government election. The data is licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. Exact source details,
the retrieval time, and a source checksum are recorded in
`brisbane_boundaries_metadata.json`.
